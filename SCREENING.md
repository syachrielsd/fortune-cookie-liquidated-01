# Screening — Fortune Cookie for the Liquidated Trader

Mission: `u-clever-harbor-75-fortune-cookie-for-the-liquidated-38fd239c`
Judge criteria, most important first:
1. Fortune-cookie voice + liquidated crypto trader + loss all present.
2. Makes the liquidated trader laugh, not cry: rueful and warm, never cruel or smug.
3. One line, at most 20 words, reads whole in a single breath.
4. Fresh joke: a turn, a double meaning, or a surprising last word.
5. Keeps the fortune voice: small, oracular, slightly formal phrasing the punchline breaks.

## Head-to-head: top 3 (all 24/25)

### #2 vs #10
Both open with a classic fortune trope and reframe it in the last clause. #10's kicker
(a fee discount on the *next* liquidation) is dark and sharp, but its target is the
exchange mocking the trader, which is a shade colder. #2's kicker (your sleep since
Monday) names the trader's own human consequence and lets him laugh at himself with
the cookie, which is exactly criterion 2's "rueful and warm". #2 also carries three
specifics (0, 0, 0 plus Monday) against #10's two. **#2 wins.**

### #2 vs #13
#13's $3.47 is a superb specific and its reframe is just as clean. But the
"inherit great wealth" fortune is a known cookie trope, so #13 loses on criterion 4's
freshness: a judge who has seen a hundred fortunes has seen the inheritance gag. #2's
triple-zero lucky numbers are a fresher turn on the same trope, and its final clause
(sleep since Monday) is the funniest part of the line, reframing the numbers as the
trader's biography. **#2 wins.**
